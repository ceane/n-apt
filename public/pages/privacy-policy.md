# Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how N-APT handles information when you use the application.

## Information we may collect

- Account and authentication data needed to operate the auth flow.
- Session and device state necessary to keep the application working.
- Information you choose to provide through the app.
- Basic operational logs generated by the application or server infrastructure for security, debugging, or reliability purposes.
- This app does not collect telemetry or crash reports.

## How we use information

- Authenticate you and maintain sessions.
- Deliver core application functionality.
- Diagnose bugs, improve performance, and secure the service.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## What we do not collect

- No telemetry.
- No crash reports.
- No ad identifiers or advertising profile data.
- No intentional collection of personal information beyond what is needed to operate authentication and the app.

## California privacy rights

If California privacy law applies to you, you may have rights to know, access, correct, and delete certain personal information, as well as rights related to disclosure and sale/sharing where applicable.

## GDPR rights

If the GDPR applies to you, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, subject to applicable exceptions.

## Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system is completely secure.

## Contact

If you have privacy questions or want to exercise your rights, open an issue or discussion on the [n-apt repository](https://github.com/ceane/n-apt), or reach the maintainer through the contact listed on the [repository owner profile](https://github.com/ceane).
